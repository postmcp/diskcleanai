# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience, give and accept
constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people using the app.
Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information
are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@diskcleanai.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly
and handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so, following the
Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
